## 2. Prediction out of sample

1-second grid. Features use only events up to t; the label is the mid-price change over (t, t+h] in ticks. Walk-forward by half-hour block (train on all earlier blocks, test on the next, 11 test blocks), with training rows whose label reaches into the test block dropped. R² is against a forecast of no change. Hit rate counts only intervals where the mid moved. 95% intervals from a 5-minute moving-block bootstrap.

## 6. Market making with queue position (INTC, MSFT)

A maker quotes 100 shares at the best bid and the best ask, rejoins at the back of the queue after each fill, and holds at most 1,000 shares either way. Queue position is tracked order by order from the LOBSTER order IDs under first-in-first-out priority (see `src/lobofi/maker.py`). Each fill is marked to the mid-price h seconds later, in ticks per share; positive is good for the maker. 'Edge at fill' is the half-spread earned against the mid just before the fill. Nasdaq paid makers a rebate of 0.20 to 0.295 ticks a share in 2012, so a fill breaks even after rebate when its markout is above -0.295 at the top tier.
